#### ==Adoption is a people problem==, not a tooling one.

I ran an AI adoption programme across seven business functions. The hard part
was never the tool. It was finding, inside each function, the one task people
already hated, and starting there.

I shipped 10 automations in Excel VBA, Power Query and Power BI for Finance,
Marketing, PR and Operations, removing **40+ hours** of manual work a month.
One is a routing engine that assigns 22 sales reps across 760 stores, replacing
a territory assignment someone was doing by hand. Training reached **86**
people and scored above **4.7/5**.

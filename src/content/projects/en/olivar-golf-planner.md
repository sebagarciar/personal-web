A one-page app I open the night before a round at Golf Olivar de la Hinojosa
(Madrid), aiming to beat my best score of 98. It turns the club's own course
guide into a video-game-style map and picks a club per hole from my own bag,
scoring water, bunkers and OB inside each landing zone. A target-score slider
spreads the extra strokes over the hardest holes first. Built 2026-09-24, not
yet tested on the course.

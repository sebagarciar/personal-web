Our household runs across Spain and Chile, on two currencies and two banking
systems that no budgeting app handles. So I built one. It parses statements from
three banks, categorises **1,816** real transactions on its own and learns from
my corrections. In daily use since June 2026.

The figures in the screenshot are demo data. The app runs on mine.

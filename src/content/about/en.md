I've been the family's tech person since I was a teenager, always chasing the
newest gadget. That curiosity is why I studied ==engineering== with a minor in
==IT==, but it's also where I realized the software engineer path wasn't for
me.

I wanted to keep building my skills, so I joined Simon-Kucher as a consultant.
There, I restructured pricing for a local business and watched the
recommendation move their growth. That's when the goal stopped being the
numbers and became the ==impact== behind them.

Consulting was missing the technology side, so I moved to Uber next. There, I
led end-to-end projects and saw how technology shapes everyday life. It's the
same pull I feel toward ==AI== today.

I'm still the person people come to with a tech problem, just with better
tools now. The goal hasn't changed: keep building toward ==leadership roles==
at major companies back in Chile.

For now, though, I love where ==technology== and ==business== meet. That's
why I'm looking for a role that needs both, where carrying a number and
building the thing that moves it are the same job.

Outside work, you'll find me on a golf course or watching soccer. Travel is
my real passion: I've visited more than 40 countries so far.
